Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is the purpose of this portal?
This portal helps students manage their academic activities such as timetable, assignments, and results.
2. How can I reset my password?
Click on the 'Forgot Password' link on the login page and follow the steps.
3. Where can I see my attendance?
You can view your attendance in the 'Attendance' section after logging in.
4. How can I contact support?
You can contact our support team at support@portal.edu or via the ‘Help’ menu in your dashboard.
